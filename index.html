QUESTIONS


How much can I earn?
10 WORK per hour for every staked NPC — 240 WORK per day per NPC. Stake 5 NPCs and that is 1,200 WORK a day. Use the calculator in the staking section above to try your own numbers.


When can I claim, and what does it cost?
As soon as you have earned at least 10 WORK. A claim costs a flat 0.00022 BNB fee, and the WORK lands in your wallet in the same transaction — no queues and no manual payouts.


Are my NPCs locked when I stake?
Never. Unstaking is free at any time, and unstaking never touches the rewards you have already earned — they stay claimable.


Who can mint a BEWORK NPC?
Any wallet holding a Binance Agent ID. The first 2 mints per wallet are free, then $1 each, up to 12 per wallet. Every NPC can be staked to earn WORK.
