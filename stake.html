03 — STAKE NFTS

First time only: approve the staking contract to move your NFTs (one approval). Then enter the token IDs to stake, separated by commas — e.g. 12, 305, 1999. Make sure the IDs belong to this wallet.

04 — UNSTAKE NFTS

Take your NFTs back at any time — no fee, never locked. Rewards you already earned stay recorded and can be claimed afterwards.

05 — CLAIM WORK

Claiming sends WORK directly to your wallet in this very transaction. Each claim attaches a fee of 0.00022 BNB (read live from the contract) and needs accrued rewards of at least the minimum claim shown in the panel.

Staking runs through the BeworkStaking contract; the BEWORK NFT contract is unchanged.
