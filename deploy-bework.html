BEWORK — Token + Staking Deploy Console (Owner)

Owner-only page: deploy the BEWORK (WORK) token and the BeworkStaking contract so BEWORK NFT holders can stake their NFTs and claim WORK. Every step is signed by the owner wallet — nothing runs automatically.

  • This package is a separate new set of contracts: the WORK token + a staking contract. The BEWORK NFT contract is not modified in any way — staking works through standard ERC-721 approvals and transfers.
  • Token: BEWORK (WORK), fixed supply of 1,000,000,000, all of it minted to the deployer wallet at deployment; there is no mint or burn function afterwards, so the supply never changes. Planned allocation (executed by the owner from their wallet): 50% staking reserve (500M, step 04), 40% LP, 10% treasury.
  • Staking: reward of 10 WORK per hour per staked NFT (240 WORK/day), counted from the moment the NFT is staked — never retroactive. Claiming sends WORK directly to the claimer's wallet in the same transaction; there is no manual per-person distribution. The owner only funds the reward reserve held by the staking contract.
  • Every claim must attach a fee of 0.00022 BNB; fees accumulate in the staking contract and can be withdrawn by the owner (withdrawFees). Minimum claim is 10 WORK. Unstaking is always free and is never blocked by the reserve balance.
  • If the WORK reserve in the staking contract is smaller than someone's rewards, a claim pays what is available and the rest stays recorded for a later claim — rewards are never lost.
  • BEWORK NFT contract (already deployed, untouched): 0x78eDD42101e889cC3Ce4B24CBb0Fc774082BcAee.

Warning: these contracts are not audited. Operate only with your own wallet, in a dApp browser (OKX / MetaMask / Trust). Never share your seed phrase or private key with anyone.

02 — Deploy the BEWORK (WORK) token

Deploy the BEWORK (WORK) ERC-20 token with no constructor arguments. After deployment, all 1,000,000,000 WORK land in your wallet and the address is filled into steps 03–05 automatically.

Waiting for the wallet to connect.

03 — Deploy BeworkStaking

Deploy the staking contract. Constructor parameters: the BEWORK NFT address (prefilled), the WORK token address (automatic from step 02, editable), a rate of 10 WORK/hour per NFT, a claim fee of 0.00022 BNB, and a minimum claim of 10 WORK — the last three are baked in from this page and can be changed by the owner later via setRewardRate / setClaimFee / setMinClaim.

04 — Fund the staking reserve

Transfer WORK from your wallet into the staking contract as the reward reserve. Holders' claims are paid from this reserve — fund it once up front (per the tokenomics: 500,000,000 WORK) and top it up only when it runs low.
